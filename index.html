Cats History

All domestic cats descend from the Near Eastern wildcat (Felis silvestris lybica) and began living alongside humans roughly 10,000 to 12,000 years ago in the Fertile Crescent.

  • The First Alliance (10,000–8,000 BCE): Wild cats came to early human farms to hunt mice and rats near grain stores, forming a natural, helpful partnership.
  • Ancient Egypt (3,000 BCE): Cats became sacred, godlike figures, worshipped through the feline goddess Bastet and often mummified after death.
    • Global Travel (1000 BCE-500 CE): Ships carried cats across Asia, the Mediterranean, and Europe for pest control.
  • The Middle Age (500-1500 BCE): Europe viewed cats with fear, linking them to witchcraft and dark magic.
  • Modern Return (1600's- present): Cats regained popularity during the Enlightenment, turning into one of the world's most cherished household pets.
